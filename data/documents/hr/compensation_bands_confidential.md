# PeoplePrime Technologies Compensation Bands (Confidential, HR Managers Only)

This document is restricted to the hr-managers group. Do not share it with employees.

## Salary Bands 2026
Annual base salary bands for full-time roles, in euros:
- Band A (associate): 34,000 to 42,000 EUR
- Band B (professional): 42,000 to 55,000 EUR
- Band C (senior): 55,000 to 72,000 EUR
- Band D (lead): 72,000 to 90,000 EUR
- Band E (head of department): 90,000 to 120,000 EUR

## Annual Raise Budget
The 2026 merit raise budget is 3.5 percent of the payroll of each department. Managers may propose individual raises between 0 and 8 percent; any raise above 8 percent requires approval by the HR director.

## Promotion Rules
A promotion to the next band comes with a raise of at least 7 percent, or the minimum of the new band if higher. Promotions are reviewed twice a year, in April and October.

## Confidentiality
Individual salaries and band assignments are confidential. Managers must not disclose another employee's band or salary. Questions from employees about their own band should be redirected to their HR business partner.
